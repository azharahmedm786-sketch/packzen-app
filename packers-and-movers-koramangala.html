Koramangala is laid out in numbered Blocks (1st through 8th), and is one of Bangalore's most concentrated hubs for startups, young professionals, and shared housing — which means a large share of our Koramangala bookings are smaller moves: single rooms, PG shifts, and compact 1BHK or 2BHK apartments, often on a tighter budget and timeline than a full-house relocation elsewhere.

The Blocks vary quite a bit in character, from the busy commercial stretch around 80 Feet Road and Sony World Junction to the quieter, more residential inner Blocks. We size vehicles and crews to match — a single-room PG move doesn't need the same setup as a family relocating out of a 3BHK in the same area.

Moving in Koramangala: What to Know

  • Koramangala has a high volume of PG and single-room moves alongside full apartment relocations — our quote tool prices these very differently, so you're not paying for a full-house move you don't need.
  • Traffic and parking around the 80 Feet Road / Sony World Junction commercial stretch is tighter than the inner residential Blocks; we plan loading windows accordingly.
