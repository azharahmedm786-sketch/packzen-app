PackZen's house shifting service in Bangalore is built around that reality, not just around trucks and labour.

Pricing, Explained Simply

House shifting costs in Bangalore depend mainly on your home size (1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK, villa), the distance between your old and new address, floor access at both ends, and whether you want full packing included. A small 1BHK local move can start from around ₹1,999–₹3,500, while larger homes or longer distances cost more. Enter your details in the quote form to see your exact price.

What to Know Before You Book

One detail that catches a lot of people off guard during a Bangalore house shift is floor access. A 3rd-floor flat without a lift, common in older buildings around areas like Indiranagar or Jayanagar, needs more crew and more time than the same move in a high-rise with a working service lift, and pricing should reflect that honestly rather than surprising you with an extra 'labour charge' on moving day. We factor floor and lift access into your quote upfront specifically so there's no renegotiation once your furniture is already on the stairs.

The other common pain point is the gap between your old home's move-out date and your new home's move-in readiness. If you need short-term storage between the two, or a split move across two days because your new flat's registration or painting isn't finished yet, mention it when booking, our team can plan around a staggered handover rather than forcing everything into a single same-day move that doesn't actually fit your situation.
