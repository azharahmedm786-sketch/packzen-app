Electronic City is one of Bangalore's oldest and largest dedicated IT hubs, split across Phase 1 and Phase 2, and connected to the rest of the city primarily via the Electronic City elevated expressway. Its somewhat separate location, south of the main city and a fair distance from the HSR/Koramangala belt, means moves in or out of the area benefit from a crew that already knows the expressway access points and each phase's internal layout.

A large share of our Electronic City bookings are tech employees relocating for a new job or a company shift between the two phases, along with office relocations for companies moving within or into the tech park campuses. We plan vehicle routing around expressway toll points and peak-hour congestion at the entry/exit ramps.

Moving in Electronic City: What to Know

  • Electronic City is split into Phase 1 and Phase 2 with distinct access roads — let us know which phase you're in so we route the vehicle correctly from the start.
  • The elevated expressway is the main access route; we plan around its peak-hour congestion, particularly for moves timed around the morning and evening office commute windows.
