Indiranagar is one of Bangalore's older, more established neighbourhoods — tree-lined streets, a mix of independent houses and low-rise apartments, and narrower internal lanes than you'll find in the newer eastern suburbs. Its central location, close to the CBD and well connected to both the airport road and the ORR, makes it a popular choice for professionals who want a shorter commute, and a fairly steady source of both local and cross-city moving bookings.

Because a meaningful share of Indiranagar properties are older independent houses rather than gated apartment complexes, our crews plan for narrower street access and on-street loading rather than assuming a basement parking bay or dedicated loading dock will be available.

Moving in Indiranagar: What to Know

  • Many Indiranagar homes are independent houses on narrower lanes, particularly in the older blocks (1st Stage through HAL 2nd Stage) — smaller vehicles are often used for the final approach even on larger moves.
  • Parking near the commercial stretches (100 Feet Road, CMH Road) can be limited during the day; we plan loading windows around this where the move touches those areas.
