What to Know Before You Book

The most common packing mistake we see in DIY moves isn't fragile items, most people are already careful with those, it's the boring stuff: kitchen boxes packed too heavy to lift safely, books stacked in a way that collapses the box bottom, and clothes crammed loosely so hangers and drawers arrive as a tangled mess. A trained packer knows to distribute weight across smaller boxes, reinforce bottoms on anything dense, and use wardrobe boxes for hanging clothes specifically so unpacking isn't its own multi-hour project.

Unpacking gets skipped more often than it should, mostly because by the time boxes arrive at the new place, everyone's exhausted and it feels easier to 'deal with it later.' Later often becomes weeks. If you book unpacking support alongside packing, our team places items by room according to your labelling and can do basic setup, kitchen items into cabinets, clothes into wardrobes, so your new home is actually livable on day one instead of a maze of sealed boxes.
