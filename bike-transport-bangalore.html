Moving a motorcycle or scooter isn't the same job as moving furniture — it needs proper tie-down, careful loading, and a driver who understands that a scratched tank or bent lever is a real loss, not a minor inconvenience. PackZen's bike transport service in Bangalore is built for exactly that, whether you're shifting across the city or sending your bike to another city entirely.

For local Bangalore transport, your bike is securely loaded onto our vehicle, strapped down to prevent movement during transit, and delivered directly to your new address — no need to ride it across the city yourself during a hectic moving day. For intercity transport, we use appropriate crating and tie-down methods suited to longer road journeys, so your bike arrives in the same condition it left in.

This service is popular with people relocating homes within Bangalore who don't want to add a bike ride to an already packed moving day, as well as students and professionals relocating between cities who need their two-wheeler to arrive safely without riding it themselves over long distances.

You can book bike transport as a completely standalone service, without booking any other move through PackZen, or add it to a house or office relocation you're already arranging with us. Either way, pricing and pickup are handled the same straightforward way: enter your locations, get an instant quote, and schedule a pickup window that works for you.

What to Know Before You Book

A lot of bike transport requests we get in Bangalore come from people who've just bought a new home outside their old neighbourhood and don't want to navigate rush-hour traffic, especially areas like Silk Board or the ORR stretch, on a fully loaded moving day when they're also trying to be at the new place to receive furniture. Sending the bike ahead separately means one less thing to juggle, and one less risk of an accident while distracted by everything else moving day involves.

For intercity transport, the bigger question people ask is what actually happens to the bike during the journey, whether it's just tied loosely in the back of a generic truck, or handled with a process. We use anchoring specific to two-wheelers rather than treating a bike like just another piece of cargo, and every transport includes a pre-pickup condition check with photos, so if anything looks different at delivery, there's already a clear reference point rather than a dispute based on memory.
