What to Know Before You Book

Families relocating within Bangalore often have this exact problem: two working adults, one car each, and only one of you can realistically drive during the move because someone needs to be at the new place to direct furniture placement and receive deliveries. Rather than asking a friend or relative to drive your second car across the city, or leaving it parked at the old address for days until someone finds time, sending it separately means it arrives on your schedule, not whenever you can free up a driver.

For moves to another city entirely, the math is simpler: a long personal drive adds hours of fatigue, fuel cost, toll charges, and wear on a vehicle you're trying to keep in good condition, on top of everything else an intercity relocation already demands. Transporting the car instead means you can fly, take a train, or focus on the rest of the move, while your vehicle arrives separately, inspected and accounted for.
