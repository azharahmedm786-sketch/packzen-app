Malleshwaram is one of Bangalore's oldest and best-loved neighbourhoods, known for its numbered cross roads, heritage homes and busy market streets around Sampige Road. Moves here often involve older houses, narrow approaches and furniture that has been in the family for years.

We take extra care with heavy wooden pieces and plan access ahead, since some lanes are narrow and market areas get crowded. Where a large truck can't reach the door, we use a smaller vehicle or a short carry.

Moving in Malleshwaram: What to Know

  • Heritage homes often have heavy, older furniture; mark these items so we bring the right padding and crew.
  • Market streets get crowded later in the day; morning loading is usually easier.
  • Narrow lanes may need a smaller vehicle, which we confirm before your move.
