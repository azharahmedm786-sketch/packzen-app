Why partner with PackZen

  • Parcel deliveries within Bangalore (up to 20 kg)

How it works

  1. Register in about 5 minutes and upload your documents.
  2. Get verified — our team reviews your application, usually within 1–2 working days, and you get an SMS.
  3. Go online in the PackZen crew app and accept jobs that match your skills and area.
  4. Finish the job with the customer's completion code and see your earnings in the app.

What you need

  • A two-wheeler with valid RC
  • A valid driving licence
  • Aadhaar and PAN
  • A smartphone with internet

Questions

What do I deliver?

Documents, boxes and small items up to 20 kg, picked up and dropped within Bangalore.

How is delivery confirmed?

The receiver gets a delivery code by SMS and gives it to you when they receive the parcel.

How much do I earn?

You keep 90% of each delivery's value, shown in the PackZen crew app.
