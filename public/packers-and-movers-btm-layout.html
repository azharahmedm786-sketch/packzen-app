BTM Layout, split into 1st and 2nd Stage near Silk Board junction, is one of south Bangalore's busiest residential areas for students and young professionals. A large share of our BTM bookings are PG moves, single rooms and compact 1BHK shifts.

Narrow inner roads and limited parking are common in BTM, so we often use smaller vehicles and plan loading points with you before the move. Small moves can qualify for our mini move price when the load is light and the distance is short.

Moving in BTM Layout: What to Know

  • Small PG and single-room moves may qualify for the ₹1,499 mini move price (up to 5 items, within 10 km, no packing).
  • Silk Board junction traffic can add time; we plan pickup windows to avoid peak hours where possible.
  • Narrow lanes may need a smaller vehicle or a short carry to the truck, which we confirm in advance.
