Questions

How much does a website cost?

Starter websites start at ₹7,999 and Business websites at ₹14,999. Booking or online-store websites start at ₹29,999. Your final price is confirmed in writing after a free consultation. Domain and hosting are billed at actual cost.

How long does it take?

Typically 7–10 days for Starter, 10–15 days for Business and 15–21+ days for Advanced — once we have your content and photos.

Will you guarantee Google rankings or leads?

No — nobody honestly can. We build fast, mobile-friendly, properly structured websites and set up Google Business Profile and analytics so you're in the best position to compete.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds of revisions for Starter and Business, three for Advanced. Extra changes are quoted separately or covered by the Care Plan.

Do I own the website and domain?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the content belongs to you.
