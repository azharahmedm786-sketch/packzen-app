How it works

  1. Register in about 5 minutes and upload your documents.

What you need

Questions

How do I get jobs?

After approval, go online in the PackZen crew app. Jobs that match your vehicle and area are offered to you; you have a few minutes to accept.

How much do I earn?

You keep 90% of each job's value. PackZen keeps 10% for bringing the customer, payments and support. Cash you collect from the customer is adjusted against your payout.

Can I bring my whole team?

Yes. Register as Packers & Movers or Loading Team and tell us your crew size.
