An office move that drags into a second working day costs more than the moving bill — it costs client calls missed, meetings rescheduled, and a team that can't find their monitors. PackZen's office relocation service in Bangalore is built around one goal: getting your team from the old address to the new one with the shortest possible downtime.

We move offices, co-working spaces, retail outlets, warehouses, and small commercial setups across the city — from a 10-desk startup floor to a full warehouse of inventory racks. Office relocation costs depend on the number of workstations, volume of furniture and equipment, distance between locations, and whether the move happens during business hours or after hours. A small office move can start in a similar range to a large home move, while multi-floor or warehouse relocations are quoted based on a full inventory. What to Know Before You Book

Bangalore's commercial buildings, especially the larger tech-park campuses around Whitefield, Electronic City, and Outer Ring Road, often have their own loading-dock schedules, security clearance requirements, and vehicle-entry permits that need to be arranged in advance, sometimes 24–48 hours ahead through the building's facilities team. Missing that step is one of the most common reasons office moves get delayed by hours on moving day. We ask for your building's access requirements upfront so the right paperwork and vehicle passes are sorted before your crew shows up, not after.

IT equipment is the other area where generic movers tend to fall short. Desktops, monitors, and networking hardware need to be powered down, cable-labelled, and packed in a way that survives a truck ride without static damage or a cracked screen, and reassembling a 20-desk workstation layout correctly at the new office takes coordination, not just muscle. Our teams work from your floor plan where provided, so workstations and equipment land in the right place the first time instead of becoming a second move within the new office.
