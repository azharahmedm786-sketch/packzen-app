Modern homes in Bangalore are filled with engineered wood, modular wardrobes, and hydraulic storage beds that simply cannot fit through apartment doorways, staircases, or passenger lifts in one piece. Forcing assembled furniture through narrow corridors leads to chipped laminates, bent frames, and damaged walls.

PackZen provides professional furniture dismantling and reassembly support to make your relocation seamless. Our trained carpenters know how modern fittings, cam-lock screws, sliding hinges, and pneumatic lifts work. We systematically take pieces apart, wrap each panel in protective padding, and label all hardware so reassembly at your destination is smooth and precise.

Whether you need a 4-door sliding wardrobe unbolted, a hydraulic king bed dismantled, or modular office workstations disassembled and reconstructed, our team brings the proper tools to complete the job cleanly.

How It Works

1. Inspection & Hardware Tagging

We check existing joints and fittings, then sort bolts, screws, and brackets into secure, labelled pouches.

2. Systematic Dismantling

Panels are carefully unbolted in the correct structural order to avoid straining hinges or joints.

3. Protective Transit Packing

Each component, headboard, and panel is padded with corrugated sheets and bubble wrap for transport.

4. Reassembly & Leveling

At your new home, our carpenters reassemble the furniture, align sliding tracks, and verify balance.

What to Know Before You Book

To ensure a speedy and efficient dismantling process, we recommend clearing all personal belongings, clothing, and bed linen from wardrobes and bed storage boxes before the team arrives. This allows our carpenters to start unbolting immediately without handling personal items.

If you have bespoke or imported modular furniture that requires specific Allen key sizes or manufacturer assembly guides, let our team know when booking so our carpenters arrive with the exact tools needed for your setup.
