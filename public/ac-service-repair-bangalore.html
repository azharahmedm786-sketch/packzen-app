Good to Know

If your AC is cooling poorly, a deep service often helps more than a gas refill — low cooling is frequently caused by dirty coils rather than missing gas. If the technician finds a leak, it should be fixed before the gas is topped up, otherwise you'll lose it again.

For installations, keep the wall location and power point ready, and tell us if the outdoor unit needs to go on a high or hard-to-reach wall so the technician comes prepared.
