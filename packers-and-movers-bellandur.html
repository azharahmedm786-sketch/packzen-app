Bellandur sits on the Outer Ring Road between Sarjapur Road and Marathahalli, and has grown rapidly into a dense cluster of large high-rise apartment complexes serving the surrounding IT corridor. Most homes here are in sizeable gated communities rather than independent houses, which means moves typically involve lift booking, security check-in, and a designated loading bay rather than simple street-side loading.

Because the ORR through Bellandur is one of the more congested stretches in the city during peak hours, we plan pickup and drop timing around it where your schedule allows, and coordinate lift and loading-dock slots with your society management ahead of moving day so the crew isn't waiting at the gate.

Moving in Bellandur: What to Know

  • Almost all Bellandur bookings are large gated high-rise communities requiring lift booking and a designated loading slot, which we coordinate with society management in advance.
